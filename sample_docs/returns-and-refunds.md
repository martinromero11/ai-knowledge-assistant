# Northwind Outfitters: Returns and Refunds Policy

Last updated: March 2026

## Return window

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Members of the Northwind Trail Club get an extended 60-day return window on all purchases. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all tags.

Clearance items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged. Gift cards, custom-embroidered gear, and opened fuel canisters are also non-returnable for safety and hygiene reasons.

## How to start a return

Start a return from the Orders page in your account, or contact support with your order number. You will receive a prepaid return label by email within one business day. Returns must be shipped within 14 days of the label being issued, or the label expires.

In-store returns are accepted at any Northwind retail location. Bring the item and your order confirmation email or receipt.

## Refund timing

Once the warehouse receives a return, it is inspected within 3 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 7 business days after approval, depending on the bank. Refunds for orders paid with store credit are returned as store credit.

Original shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to our error, such as a damaged, defective, or incorrect item.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery and include photos of the item and packaging. We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, including shipping. Defects that appear later are covered by the manufacturer warranty; see the Warranty page for details.

## Exchanges

We offer free exchanges for a different size or color of the same item, subject to stock. If the replacement costs more, the difference is charged at the time of exchange. If it costs less, the difference is refunded.

## Restocking fees

There is no restocking fee for standard returns. Large items over 50 pounds, such as kayaks and rooftop tents, have a 10% restocking fee unless the return is due to our error.
