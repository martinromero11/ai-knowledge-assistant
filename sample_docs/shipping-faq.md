# Northwind Outfitters: Shipping FAQ

## What shipping options do you offer?

Standard shipping takes 4 to 7 business days and is free on orders over $75. Orders under $75 pay a flat $6.95. Expedited shipping takes 2 to 3 business days and costs $14.95. Overnight shipping is available for orders placed before 1 p.m. Pacific time on business days and costs $29.95.

## Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship to the United States and Canada only. Canadian orders take 6 to 10 business days and may be subject to duties and taxes, which are collected by the carrier on delivery.

## When will my order ship?

Most orders leave our warehouse within 1 business day. Custom-embroidered items take an extra 3 to 5 business days to produce before they ship. During the holiday season, from late November through December, processing can take up to 2 business days.

## How do I track my order?

A tracking link is emailed as soon as your order ships. You can also find tracking details on the Orders page in your account. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update after the label is created.

## Can I change my shipping address after ordering?

Address changes are possible only before the order ships. Contact support as soon as possible. Once a package is in transit, we cannot redirect it, but you can request a hold at a carrier location through the tracking page.

## What if my package is lost?

If tracking has not updated in 7 days, or a package is marked delivered but you cannot find it, contact support. We will open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or refund once the claim is confirmed, usually within 5 business days.

## Do you ship hazardous items?

Fuel canisters and certain batteries are shipped by ground only and cannot use expedited or overnight shipping. They cannot be shipped to Canada.
