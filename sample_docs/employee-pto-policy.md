# Northwind Outfitters: Paid Time Off Policy (Employees)

## Who this applies to

This policy applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO at half the full-time rate. Seasonal staff do not accrue PTO but receive paid sick leave as required by state law.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period based on years of service:

- Less than 2 years: 15 days per year
- 2 to 5 years: 20 days per year
- More than 5 years: 25 days per year

New employees can begin using accrued PTO after completing 90 days of employment.

## Carryover and cap

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Any balance above that is forfeited on December 31. Your total balance can never exceed 1.5 times your annual accrual; once you reach the cap, accrual pauses until you use some time off.

## Requesting time off

Submit requests in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of 3 or more days. Shorter requests should be submitted at least 48 hours ahead when possible. Managers approve requests based on staffing needs. Retail staff should avoid requesting time off during the blackout period from November 20 through January 5, except for emergencies.

## Sick time

Sick days are tracked separately from PTO. Every employee receives 6 paid sick days per year, which reset on January 1 and do not carry over. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Holidays

The company observes 8 paid holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. Retail staff who work on a holiday receive 1.5 times their regular pay plus a floating holiday to use within 60 days.

## Leaving the company

Unused accrued PTO is paid out in the final paycheck. Sick days and floating holidays are not paid out.
